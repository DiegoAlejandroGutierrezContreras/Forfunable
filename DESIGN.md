## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-precision, distraction-free environment engineered for technical communities, software practitioners, and developer discourse. Drawing foundational cues from modern engineering tools like Linear, the design language rejects loud, over-gamified aesthetics in favor of clean mathematical structure, crisp borders, and dark visual depth.

The UI evokes calm competence, speed, and analytical focus. Typography and spacing dictate hierarchy, keeping decorative noise to an absolute minimum while providing unmistakable functional feedback through vivid instrumental accents.

### Design Movement & Principles
- **Minimalist & Developer-First:** Content density and typography take center stage. Structural rhythm follows strict alignment rules with zero decorative excess.
- **Deep Midnight Ambiance:** Dark slate-purple baselines eliminate OLED eye strain while retaining high contrast and textural richness.
- **Instrumental Signals:** High-frequency functional accents (electric green for forward actions, cyan for analytical metadata, purple for elevated states) guide interaction without visual chaos.

## Colors

The color palette is built around deep, violet-undertoned blacks paired with high-frequency functional accents. Tonal elevation replaces traditional drop shadows, structuring depth through calculated luminance steps.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary (`#00e676`):** The kinetic driver. Reserved exclusively for definitive interactions: upvoting, publishing threads, active status indicators, and confirmed states. Ensures WCAG 2.2 AA compliance against the dark bedrock.
- **Secondary (`#38bdf8`):** The contextual anchor. Applied to category badges, active user mentions, syntax highlights, and informational tags.
- **Tertiary (`#dfbaff`):** The ambient indicator. Used sparingly for verified credentials, elevated moderator markers, and accent accents.
- **Neutral Base (`#0d0b14`):** Canvas bedrock. Replaces flat charcoal with a cold violet cast to prevent sterile visuals.
- **Surfaces (`#13101c` and `#1a1626`):** Tiered card, modal, and popover planes.
- **Borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Structural ghost outlines providing edge clarity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, geometric attitude of **Space Grotesk** for headings and counters with the neutral utility of **Inter** for sustained reading in long discussion threads and code comments.

### Application Rules
- **Headings:** Headlines above 20px always utilize `Space Grotesk` with tight negative letter spacing to craft compact visual blocks.
- **Discussion Bodies:** Core replies and markdown documents rely on `body-md` (14px/22px) and `body-lg` (16px/26px), prioritizing vertical line height to reduce visual fatigue during dense technical debates.
- **System Labels & Metadata:** Metrics, vote tallies, timestamps, and chips use `Inter` with medium (500) and semibold (600) weights to guarantee instant legibility at compact dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an asymmetrical multi-column structure designed for content discovery, discussion density, and auxiliary context.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** Three-column framework featuring a persistent navigation rail (240px), a focused primary feed/discussion track (max 760px), and an auxiliary right column (300px) for community rules, live statistics, and filtering.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Collapses the auxiliary column into context sheets or drawers while maintaining the central thread feed and a condensed icon-based sidebar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A single full-bleed vertical column using `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Navigation moves to a fixed top bar and a gesture-driven drawer.
- **Comment Tree Layout:** Nested replies indent by `1rem` (16px) per hierarchy level, bounded at a maximum of 5 indentations on mobile to preserve layout integrity.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally discards diffuse drop shadows in favor of tonal surface stepping, refined 1px border framing, and translucent backdrops.

### Surface Tiers
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure foundation at `#0d0b14`. Negative space background behind feed items.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#13101c` bounded by a 1px border of `border-subtle` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). On hover, borders brighten to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Tooltips & Menus):** `#1a1626` bounded by `border-strong` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`) paired with a high-falloff directional shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Navigation):** Translucent backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) filled with `rgba(19, 16, 28, 0.85)` and framed by `border-subtle`.
- **Focus Indicators:** Unblurred 1px outer ring in `#00e676` spaced by a 2px offset in `#0d0b14` for complete keyboard focus visibility.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances modern soft corners with utilitarian stability (`roundedness: 2`).

### Corner Geometry
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** Configured with 8px radius (`0.5rem`), matching the ergonomic reach of standard pointer devices.
- **Containers, Cards, and Modals:** Standardized on 16px radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`) to enclose dense text content comfortably.
- **Tags, Micro-Badges, and Chips:** Set to 6px radius to maintain a crisp box silhouette adjacent to typography.
- **Avatars:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) for individual members, or 8px rounded squares for organization badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#00e676`, text `#0d0b14`, font-weight 600, border radius 8px (`rounded`). Hover: `#10b981`. Active: `transform: scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#1a1626`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#f3f0fa`. Hover: border transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#9c94b0`. Hover: text `#f3f0fa` with a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill.

### Voting Widget (Linear Pill)
- Integrated pill unit set to `#13101c` with a 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Upvote trigger: Chevron icon transitioning to `#00e676` when toggled; tally shifts to `#00e676`.
- Downvote trigger: Chevron icon transitioning to `#f43f5e` when active; tally shifts to `#f43f5e`.

### Cards & Feed Items
- Background `#13101c`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, 16px border radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Padding: 16px (`space-md`).
- Integrated hover state: subtle border luminance shift (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`) without card displacement.

### Input Fields & Text Editors
- Background `#0d0b14`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border radius 8px, text `#f3f0fa`.
- Placeholder: `#605874`.
- Focus: Border shifts directly to `#00e676` with zero diffuse glow.

### Chips & Tags
- Metadata and category badges use an alpha tint of the secondary accent: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.1)` background, `#38bdf8` text, 6px border radius, and `label-sm` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox: 18x18px box with 4px border radius. Unchecked: `#1a1626` with 1px border. Checked: `#00e676` fill with `#0d0b14` check icon.

### Thread Connector Lines
- Nested reply branches display a 1px left guide line in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Hovering over a parent response highlights its connector line in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)` to trace discussion paths.
